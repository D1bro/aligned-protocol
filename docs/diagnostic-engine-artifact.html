Aligned · Internal Spec

Diagnostic Engine

Content and schema for turning a low score into a diagnosed, specific next action — evidence-based, not one-answer branching, pressure-tested against three deliberately different areas.

Status: approved for V1 Schema: not yet applied Areas tested: 3 of 10 Bands tested: Stabilise only
01 — Why this exists

One model, not ten

Every life area needs the same underlying capability: turn a low score into something more useful than a generic tip. Rather than hand-author ten separate systems, the idea was pressure-tested against three deliberately different areas — one that reduces to arithmetic (Money), one driven by external situations (Relationships), and one internal and self-reported (Confidence) — until a single model held for all three.

“Aligned should not rush to explain the user to themselves. It should help the user gather enough information to understand themselves more accurately.”

02 — The core rule

The compressed logic the schema implements

Priority decides where Aligned focuses.
Band decides the posture once that area is selected.
Diagnostic identifies what may be happening.
Evidence increases or decreases confidence in that interpretation.
Interpretation determines the next appropriate intervention.
Behaviour and review update the interpretation.
Note the correction this makes precise: it's the priority score — importance × distance from full alignment — that decides which area gets attention. The raw satisfaction score only sets the band, the level of support appropriate once an area is already selected.

04 — Severity bands

What kind of help, not just how much

Only Stabilise has been designed in depth so far. Establish through Maintain/Expand are a working hypothesis.

BandScorePosture
Stabilise1–3Diagnose before prescribing. Establish facts and context before recommending any change.
Establish4–5Build a basic structure or habit where essentially none exists yet.
Improve6–7Sharpen and add consistency to a foundation that's already there.
Optimise8–9Refine, add nuance, remove friction.
Maintain / Expand10Protect what's working; look outward from this area to others.
05 — Evidence, not branching

Answers → evidence → interpretation → route

A single answer-to-action lookup breaks the moment an outcome depends on several answers together — the normal case, not the exception. "Low confidence + unfamiliar situation + little experience + confidence improves with preparation" doesn't come from any one answer; it comes from several small weighted rules pointing the same direction. No inference engine or LLM needed for V1 — it's arithmetic over authored weights.

Reported pattern

What the client says is happening — their raw answers.

Evidence rule

A weighted link from one answer to one interpretation — supports or contradicts.

Interpretation

The highest-scoring candidate becomes the client's working interpretation.

Confidence

Belongs to the client's specific instance, not the library entry — low, medium, or high.

Confirming it: the client is asked directly — Yes, that feels accurate / Partly / No, that doesn't feel right — and the answer becomes a new evidence point. A "no" isn't argued with; it routes back into clarifying questions.

06 — Branching

Two layers before anything gets prescribed

Layer 1 · Initial classification

Score + band + first question

Sorts the client into a broad reported pattern.

“Context-dependent confidence” — solid in some areas, not in others.
↓ tests the pattern before acting on it
Layer 2 · Cause clarification

A further question, or an observation task

Accumulates evidence toward a specific interpretation — or, sometimes, another diagnostic rather than an action yet.

“Confidence holds up when prepared and experienced, drops in unfamiliar situations — likely an exposure/practice gap, not a general self-belief problem.”
07 — Action types & routing

Not every next step is a checklist — and not every one is self-guided

TypeAsks of the clientExample
Immediate diagnosticAnswer a short structured set, onceMoney Reality Check
Structured reflectionAnswer open reflection questionsRelationship Reality Check
Behavioural actionDo one defined thingHave one intentional conversation
Observation taskNotice and log over a set windowNotice self-doubt triggers, 7 days
Repeated practiceDo a small thing several timesThree graduated exposure actions
Tracking taskLog a simple measure dailyRecord sleep for 7 days
Decision taskChoose between named optionsChoose your primary financial priority
ReviewLook back and re-interpretReview what changed after 30 days

An action involving another person — a coach conversation, contacting a creditor, a health check — doesn't need a ninth type. Any action can carry requires_external_person = true; it stays behavioural, the flag changes the UX around it. Every action also carries a routing_level, so the copy and behaviour at each level stay editable, not hardcoded:

Routing levelMeaning
normalThe default — an ordinary recommended action.
support_recommendedThe action nudges toward outside help without alarm.
professional_supportThe app should be explicit this is better handled with a professional.
urgent_supportReserved for anything that shouldn't wait on the ordinary loop.
08 — Worked examples

Stabilise band, condensed

Money & Stability

2 / 10 · diagnosed by arithmetic

First action: a 30-Day Money Reality Check — available cash, confirmed 30-day income, essential 30-day outgoings, resulting surplus or shortfall. Classifies directly: surplus → organise it; small shortfall → close the gap; serious shortfall → protect essentials and create income; debt/arrears → protect essentials first (support_recommended). A rare case where the evidence is arithmetic rather than another question.

Relationships & Connection

2 / 10 · diagnosed by situation

First question sorts into a reported pattern — not enough connection, connection without depth, one difficult relationship, withdrawal, repeating unhealthy patterns, neglect through lack of time, unclear what's wanted. For "one difficult relationship," the second pass is itself still diagnostic before resolving into conversation, boundary, distance, or reconsideration.

Confidence & Self-Belief

2 / 10 · resolved by evidence

The reported pattern is the client's own theory about themselves, and may be incomplete. The context-dependent branch is where evidence-based resolution proved necessary: experience, preparation, familiarity, who's watching, stakes, expected judgement, and prior success all accumulate weight toward exposure_practice_gap versus global_self_belief_deficit — no single answer decides it.

09 — Two pattern sentences

“We understand the territory” vs. “we understand you”

The generic band interpretation is shown immediately after the Audit. A separate, personalised insight is shown once the diagnostic resolves — the moment the system demonstrates it learned something specific, not just recited a category. Kept as separate fields (client_insight on the band-level pattern, versus internal_interpretation plus rendered copy on each interpretation) so internal reasoning never accidentally surfaces as clinical-sounding client copy.

10 — Schema

Content library vs. client runtime

Content tables are authored once per area/band, editable without a redeploy, same spirit as the existing life_areas table. Runtime tables record what a specific client actually experienced. Full DDL lives in docs/diagnostic-engine.md — this is the shape, not the file.

Content / libraryHolds
bandsThe five severity bands — label and posture text.
interventionsOne row per area + band: the entry diagnostic, including the generic pattern sentence.
intervention_questions / _optionsOrdered, branchable questions and their choices.
interpretationsThe library of possible meanings — client insight, internal reasoning, default confidence, next step.
evidence_rulesWeighted answer → interpretation links (supports / contradicts).
actionsThe recommend-next content — type, config, routing level, person-flag.
routing_rulesCopy and meaning for each routing level.
Client / runtimeHolds
client_diagnostic_sessions / _responsesOne pass through an intervention, and the raw answers given.
client_interpretations"For this client, we think X, with this confidence" — superseded, not overwritten.
client_action_instancesA recommended action presented to a client, and their response to it.
client_observationsLogged occurrences for an observation-type action.
goals (altered)Gains source_action_id — "this goal emerged from this action," not "this action is the goal."
reviewsWhat happened, what worked, what got in the way.
11 — V1 build decisions

Resolved before implementation

1

Suggestions, never auto-assigned goals

Accept / Personalise / Choose another / Discuss with coach. A recommendation and a committed goal stay separate objects, linked by goals.source_action_id.

2

No content editor yet

Three areas × one band isn't enough to know what an authoring UI needs. Content ships as seeded rows until the real authoring patterns are visible.

3

Two pattern sentences, not a rewritten one

Generic band interpretation first, personalised insight once diagnosis resolves — see section 09.

4

Evidence-based resolution

diagnostic_outcomes replaced by interpretations + evidence_rules — see section 05.
